# 6. PvP & Survival

Shadowbane has **unrestricted open-world PvP**. Almost anywhere outside safeholds, anyone can attack you.

## What happens when you die

- **Items in your inventory (and usually your gold) drop for others to loot.** **⚠ verify** the exact rules on your server.
- **Equipped gear stays on you.**
- You come back to life at your bind point or a resurrection point. A healer may also be able to resurrect you where you fell.

So: **carry little and bank often.**

## Survival habits

1. **Travel light.** Only carry consumables and the gold you actually need.
2. **Watch chat and the radar or map.** Unfamiliar names nearby usually mean danger.
3. **Know your exits.** Before you start a fight, know where the runegate, a cliff to jump from, or a friendly city is.
4. **Don't fight alone near enemy cities.**
5. **Log out safely.** Only log out inside friendly territory or a safehold.

## Solo PvP tips

- **Open with your strongest attack.** Stealth classes should open from stealth.
- **Save your escape power** (a movement speed boost, a stun break, or a stealth reset) for when you need to get out.
- **Check what the enemy is buffed with before you attack.** A target with every buff running is usually not alone.
- **Duels and scout fights:** Lakebane has an active one-on-one scene around fast scout characters. Watch replays or streams to learn how people fight.

## Group PvP tips

- **Kill the healers first.** Stop their healing and the rest of the group falls.
- **Assist-train.** Everyone targets the same enemy, called by the group leader.
- **Keep your healer alive.** Stand near them and peel attackers off them.
- **Talk on voice chat.** Call out enemy numbers, directions, and when your big cooldowns are used.
- **Protect your scouts.** The team that knows where the enemy is wins.

## Common mistakes

- Running into a fight alone while your group is still 30 yards back.
- Carrying your whole bank on your character.
- Standing still when enemies start using area-of-effect spells.
- Chasing a fleeing enemy into their own city.
