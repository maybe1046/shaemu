# 7. Guilds, Cities & Sieges

In Shadowbane, players can build cities, and other players can destroy them. That's the core of the game.

## Guilds and nations

- **A guild** is your basic group of players. It has a guild charter, a leader (the Guild Leader, or GL), and officers.
- **Nations** are alliances of guilds under one leading guild. They share a city network and fight together.
- Choosing a guild also decides who your enemies are. Pick carefully.

## Cities

1. **The Tree of Life (ToL).** Every city grows around one. Planting the tree founds the city.
2. **Ranking up.** You spend gold and resources to raise the tree's rank. Higher ranks unlock more buildings, walls, and NPCs.
3. **Buildings.** Walls, towers, shrines, banks, warehouses, and buildings for NPC craftsmen and trainers.
4. **NPCs.** Vendors, trainers, and crafting NPCs (used to roll gear). See [Economy & Crafting](08-economy-crafting.md).
5. **Upkeep.** Cities cost gold and resources to maintain. **⚠ verify** the upkeep rates on your server.

## Sieges

- **Bane.** An attacking guild places a **bane circle** (a bane stone) near an enemy city to declare war and schedule a siege. The defenders usually get some say over the time window. **⚠ verify** the scheduling rules.
- **Siege day.** Attackers break walls and buildings using siege engines and area-of-effect spells, then try to destroy the Tree of Life.
- **Outcome.** If the tree falls, the city is destroyed or captured. If the defenders hold, the bane fails.

### Siege tips

- **Attackers:** Scout the defenses beforehand, bring healers and siege specialists, and assign roles such as wall breakers, a group to protect the siege engines, and a group to kill enemy healers.
- **Defenders:** Hold the choke points, repair buildings between assaults, and don't chase attackers outside the walls.
- **Get the numbers out.** Attendance usually decides a siege. Schedule it when your players are online.

## Mines

- Mines produce valuable resources. Guilds fight over them at **scheduled mine windows**.
- **Lakebane changes:**
  - There's **no distance penalty** on mine production.
  - **Universal mines** produce any resource type, based on a fixed gold value.
  - **Mithril**, needed for Epic gems, comes **only from mines**.
  - Everyone alive and within loading range of a mine when it's claimed receives a **1M-value Glass gift**, even if their side lost. That makes it worth showing up.
- Details can change between patches. See the [patch notes](https://lakebane.com/patch_notes.html).
