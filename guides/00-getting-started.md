# 0. Getting Started

## Which server?

| | Lakebane | Shadowbane: Resurgence |
|---|---|---|
| Where | [lakebane.com](https://lakebane.com/) | [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4371680/Shadowbane_Resurgence/) |
| Install | Download the client or launcher from the site and follow its instructions | Install through Steam like any other game |
| Platform | Windows | Windows (Steam Deck support is unconfirmed) |
| Community | [Lakebane Discord](https://discord.com/invite/wQq5Eq3P63) | [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4371680/discussions/) |

Both run the classic Shadowbane rules: open-world PvP everywhere, full-loot inventories, player-built cities, and sieges. The race and class system is the same (12 races, 22 promotion classes, plus disciplines).

## Before you create a character

1. **Open the [Character Builder](https://lakebane.com/calculator/)** and sketch out 2–3 builds. You pick your race and base class at creation, and you **can't change them later**.
2. **Ask your guild or Discord what's needed.** Groups always want healers, and scouts or stealth characters are always useful.
3. **Consider a support alt.** Many players keep a second character, such as a crafter, a buffer, or a character who can open runegates, when the server rules allow it. **⚠ verify** the multiboxing rules before running more than one client.

## Your first hour

1. **Read the tooltips on your starting powers.** Put the ones you'll press constantly on keys 1–6.
2. **Remap your keys.** Useful binds to set up early:
   - Target nearest enemy, target next enemy, and assist
   - Sit or rest (you regenerate much faster while sitting)
   - Toggle run or walk
   - Screenshot and camera zoom
3. **Kill the easy monsters (mobs) near your starting area** until you reach about level 5–10. Loot everything, because early gold matters.
4. **Visit the trainers.** Spend training points on your weapon or casting skill first.
5. **Find a guild.** Ask in general chat or Discord. Look for one with an active city.

## Interface tips

- **Chat channels.** Watch the channels your guild or nation uses for warnings, and join guild voice chat if they have one.
- **Map.** Learn where the runegates, safeholds, and your guild's city are.
- **Inventory weight.** Carrying too much slows you down. Sell or bank junk.
- **Tooltips.** Hover over gear to see its stats. A gold or colored name usually means magic properties ("rolled" gear).

## Safety from day one

- **Everything in your bags can be looted if you die.** Only take what you need.
- **Bind or recall to a friendly city.** Travelling across the world with a full bag is how people lose everything.
- **Log out somewhere safe,** such as inside a friendly city or a safehold, never in the open.
