# 1. Races

Shadowbane has **12 playable races**. Your race sets your starting stats, your stat caps, some resistances and special abilities, and **which classes you can take**.

> Exact stat numbers, caps, and class restrictions differ between patches and servers. Check the [Lakebane Character Builder](https://lakebane.com/calculator/) before you create a character. **⚠ verify**

## Overview

| Race | Typical strengths | Typical weaknesses | Commonly played as |
|------|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Aelfborn** | Half-elf with balanced stats and a wide choice of classes | Doesn't excel at anything | Hybrids, rogues, casters |
| **Aracoix** | Bird-folk. Very agile, and can glide, which reduces fall damage and gives unusual travel routes | Low health (fragile) | Archers, rogues, scouts |
| **Centaur** | Fast movement and solid melee stats | Big target, can wear fewer armor slots, limited class list **⚠ verify** | Melee fighters, hunters |
| **Dwarf** | Tough, with good resistances. Strong defensive fighters and healers | Can't become a mage (arcane casting), and moves slowly | Tanks, healers, crafters |
| **Elf** | High intelligence, spirit and dexterity | Low health and strength | Mages, archers, rangers |
| **Half Giant** | Huge strength and constitution | Low dexterity and intelligence, and a very large target | Heavy melee |
| **Human** | Can take any class and gets flexible bonus stat points | No special racial abilities | Anything, and a good first character |
| **Irekei** | Desert people with good fire resistance and quick, agile fighting | **⚠ verify** racial vulnerabilities | Melee rogues and fighters, some casters |
| **Minotaur** | Very strong melee stats and good health | Fighter classes only, can't wear helmets, huge target | Barbarian or warrior bruisers |
| **Nephilim** | Descendants of angels with strong spell-casting stats | Limited class list **⚠ verify** | Casters, dark hybrids |
| **Shade** | Bonuses to stealth and hiding | Low health | Assassins, thieves, scouts |
| **Vampire** | Life drain and night-themed powers **⚠ verify** | Limited class list and racial drawbacks **⚠ verify** | Melee and hybrid killers |

## How to choose

1. **Pick your role first** (tank, healer, damage, stealth, or support), then choose the race with the best stats for it.
2. **A race's size matters in PvP.** Half Giants, Minotaurs and Centaurs are easy to click on and chase down. Small races are harder to target.
3. **If you're new, play a Human or Aelfborn.** Either can take any class (Aelfborn has nearly as many options), so a bad early decision is easier to recover from.
4. **Look at the stat caps, not just the starting numbers.** Late-game builds reach the cap on key stats using runes, so the maximum matters more than what you start with.

## Race tips

- **Aracoix:** Learn where you can glide. Jumping off a cliff is a great way to escape attackers who can't follow.
- **Shade:** Pair the racial stealth bonus with a stealth class (Assassin, Thief, Scout, or Nightstalker) to get the most out of it.
- **Dwarf:** Dwarves are among the best at soaking up damage. They're good at defending a city or holding a doorway during a siege.
- **Elf and Aracoix:** Keep your distance. Your low health punishes you in melee.
