# 8. Economy & Crafting

## Gold

- **Ways to make gold:** looting monsters, selling to NPCs, trading with players, running mines, and selling crafted gear and runes.
- **Ways gold gets spent:** training, city ranks and upkeep, gear rolls, and siege costs.
- **Bank it.** Gold in your bag can be looted if you die.

## Resources

- Resources such as ores, woods, and gems come from **mines** and from drops.
- You need them for city buildings, rank-ups, and crafting.
- On Lakebane, **Mithril comes only from mines** and is needed for **Epic gems**.

## Crafting ("rolling")

- Shadowbane crafting uses **NPC craftsmen who work in a city**.
- You give the NPC an order for an item. It comes out with random or chosen **prefixes and suffixes** that add stat bonuses. This is called "rolling" gear.
- Higher-rank crafters and cities can make better items. **⚠ verify** the rank thresholds.
- Items with top-tier rolls are the main luxury goods in the economy.

## Gems (Lakebane)

- **Gems can be socketed into equipment** to improve its stats.
- There are **3 tiers**: **Crude**, **Superior**, and **Epic**.
- Epic gems require **Mithril**, which only comes from mines.
- Each gem adds a different stat. Choose ones that fix your build's weak spots.

## Money-making tips

1. **Farm high-level camps with a group.** Rare runes and rolled items sell well.
2. **Sell discipline runes and stat runes you can't use.**
3. **Go to mine claims.** On Lakebane you get the Glass gift just for being there and alive.
4. **Buy low and sell high** between cities, but carry goods carefully because you can lose them on death.
5. **Craft for your guild.** A city with good crafters has a real advantage.
