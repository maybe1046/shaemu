# 12. Personal Build: Solo PvP Fury (new player)

**Who this is for:** a brand-new player, currently solo, who wants to hunt other players and win one-on-one fights.

**The pick:** an **Irekei Fury**, specialising in ice magic ("Ice Line").

> This build is adapted from [How to be Furious – Complete Guide to Fury](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2550905629) and the [Morloch Wiki: Fury Builds](https://morloch.shadowbaneemulator.com/index.php/Fury_Builds). Those were written for other servers. **Before you create the character, rebuild it in the [Lakebane Character Builder](https://lakebane.com/calculator/)** to confirm the race, runes and point totals are all allowed on Lakebane. **⚠ verify**

## Why Fury?

| What you want | How Fury delivers it |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Win solo fights | Fast spell damage (ice and lightning nukes), stuns, blinds and debuffs |
| Escape bad fights | **Permanent flight.** Most attackers can't follow you into the air |
| Survive while solo | Irekei racial abilities plus the Runecaster discipline give you **two heals** |
| Level alone | Area-of-effect spells and self-heals make solo farming fast |
| Stay useful later | Also strong at mines, sieges and group fights once you join a guild |

The trade-off is that you're a caster wearing cloth armour, so you're **fragile in melee**. The whole playstyle is about controlling the distance between you and your target.

## 1. Character creation

| Choice | Pick |
|--------|------|
| Race | **Irekei**: fire resistance, quick, good stats and racial disciplines |
| Gender | **Female**. Fury is female only |
| Base class | **Mage** |
| Promotion class | **Fury** (on Lakebane, promote right away at level 10) |

**Starting runes** (the extra perks you pick at character creation):
- **−5 Strength, −5 Dexterity, −5 Spirit.** You don't need these stats, and giving them up frees points for the ones you do.
- **Brilliant Mind:** more Intelligence, which powers your spells.
- **Warlord's Page**
- **Tough as Nails:** more health, because you're fragile.

**Stats:** put your points into **INT** (spell damage and mana) and **CON** (health). Put nothing extra into STR, DEX or SPI.

## 2. Training plan

At full level, the build spends about **650 training points**:

| Skill | Target | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Stormcalling** | **130 (max)** | Your main damage skill. **Train it first and always** |
| **Warding** | 60 | Magic defence |
| **Dagger** | 30 | Lets you use daggers |
| **Sorcery** | 16 | Opens up useful utility spells |

**Powers to bring to Grand Master** (spell names as listed in the source build):
- **Damage:** Ice Nuke, Lightning Nuke, Lightning AoE, Blizzard, Fury of Wind
- **Control:** Stun, Blind, Ice Debuff, Inflict Poison
- **Defence:** Shielding, Storm Ward, Elemental DA
- **Healing and utility:** the Runecaster heal (at Journeyman), and DNF

**Why ice?** An "Ice Line" Fury boosts cold-damage spells rather than lightning. Ice also comes with slows and debuffs, which is exactly what you want to keep melee attackers away from you.

**Order to train in:**
1. **Levels 10–20:** Stormcalling as high as it will go, plus a nuke and Stun.
2. **Levels 20–40:** keep raising Stormcalling, and start Warding.
3. **Levels 40+:** finish Warding, then Dagger and Sorcery. Bring the control and defensive spells up to Grand Master.

## 3. Disciplines

Get these discipline runes by farming or buying them:

1. **Rune Caster:** your second heal. Get this first, because it makes solo play much safer.
2. **Blade Weaver:** stronger dual daggers.
3. **Undead Hunter:** bonus damage against undead, which helps farm the undead zones (Greensward Pyre, Grimscairne, Bone Marches).

| Slot | Target |
|------|--------|
| Every piece | Aim for **+24 INT** rolls |
| Main hand | **165-ATR staff with +12 INT** (ATR is attack rating) |
| Head and chest | **Archmage's of Mastermind** hood and robe |
| Hands | **CC gloves** |
| Boots | **Windies with +40 stamina** |

At first, just wear the best INT and CON gear you can afford. Top gear comes later.

On Lakebane, also look at **gems**. Socket INT or CON gems into your gear; Epic gems need Mithril, which only comes from mines.

## 6. Leveling path (solo)

| Levels | Where | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| 10 | Starting area → promote to Fury | Collect the Conc pot and 1,000 gold, and spend the gold on Stormcalling |
| 10–20 | Starting area, then **Sea Dog's Rest** | Buy **CON and INT potions** there. Bank the gold you get each level |
| 20–35 | Holloch Forest or Fellgrim Forest | Pull 2–3 mobs at a time and hit them with area spells |
| 25–40 | Greensward Pyre (undead) | Good use for Undead Hunter later |
| 30–50 | Ashfell Plain, Black Bog, Blood Sands | Start taking on small PvP fights |
| 35–60 | Grimscairne, Bone Marches | Undead zones. Grimscairne sells elite gear |
| 50–70 | Plain of Ashes, Pandemonium | End-game zones. Expect other players here |
| 71–80 | Mines and PvP | You can only gain these levels through PvP |

## 7. Solo PvP playbook

### Before the fight
- **Buff up:** shields, Storm Ward, and potions.
- **Check your target:** a fully buffed player is probably not alone. A lone player who is low on health or busy fighting mobs is the ideal target.
- **Check your escape:** know where you'll fly to if it goes wrong.

### The fight (the usual order)
1. **Open from maximum range** with **Blind** or **Stun**.
2. **Put the ice debuff on them** to slow them.
3. **Burst:** Ice Nuke, then Lightning Nuke.
4. **If they get close:** stun again, **fly** to put distance between you, then start nuking again.
5. **Heal only when it's safe:** your racial and Runecaster heals are your emergency buttons.

### When to leave
- A second enemy shows up.
- Your stun is on cooldown and a melee enemy is on you.
- You're under 40% health and they aren't.

When in doubt, **fly away**. A fight you walk away from costs you nothing.

### Habits that make you better
1. **Put your keys in a fixed order:** open, debuff, nuke, nuke, stun, heal, escape. Keep that order every fight so it becomes muscle memory.
2. **Fight duels** at safe spots. Lakebane has an active duel scene, and duels are the cheapest way to practise.
3. **Watch the fights you lose.** Ask yourself what the enemy did that you didn't expect.
4. **Carry only what you can afford to lose.** Your bags can be looted when you die.
5. **Watch chat all the time,** because it's your early warning system.
