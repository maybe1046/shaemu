# 10. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **AoE** | Area-of-effect spell or attack that hits everyone in an area |
| **Assist** | Target whatever another player is targeting |
| **Bane** | Declaring a siege on a city by placing a bane stone near it |
| **Box / Boxing** | Playing several characters at once (multiboxing) |
| **CC** | Crowd control: stuns, roots, and similar effects |
| **Disc** | Discipline |
| **Gank** | Killing a lone or weaker player, often by surprise or with more people |
| **GL** | Guild Leader |
| **IC** | Inner Council (guild officers) |
| **Mob** | A computer-controlled monster |
| **Nation** | An alliance of guilds under one leading guild |
| **Peel** | Pulling attackers off an ally, usually a healer |
| **Promo** | Promotion class |
| **R1–R8** | The rank of a Tree of Life or city |
| **Roll** | Crafting an item with random magical bonuses |
| **Rune** | An item that permanently adds stats or a discipline |
| **Runegate** | A fast-travel portal |
| **Safehold** | An area where PvP isn't allowed |
| **Scout** | The class, or any player sent to gather intel on enemies |
| **ToL** | Tree of Life, the center of a city |
| **Train** | A whole group attacking one target at once |
| **Zerg** | A large, loosely organized group that wins by numbers |
