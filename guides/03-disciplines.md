# 3. Disciplines

Disciplines are extra specializations you add to your character. Each one gives new powers, skills, or stat bonuses, and they're how you fine-tune a build.

## How they work

- You get a discipline by using a **discipline rune**. These drop from monsters, can be bought from other players, or come from special content. Some are rare. **⚠ verify** where they drop on your server.
- Each discipline has **requirements**: a minimum level, stat thresholds, and allowed races and classes.
- You can only have a **limited number of disciplines** at once. **⚠ verify** the limit and whether you can remove or replace one.
- Some disciplines **can't be combined** with each other.

## Tips

- **Choose disciplines that fix your build's weaknesses.** If your character is fragile, take one that adds health. If it's slow, take one that adds speed.
- **Check a discipline's stat requirements before you allocate stats.** Missing a requirement by 1 point is a very common mistake.
- **Runes are tradeable wealth.** An expensive discipline rune you can't use is still valuable to sell.
