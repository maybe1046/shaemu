# 2. Classes

## How classes work

1. **Base class, chosen at creation.** There are four: **Fighter**, **Healer**, **Rogue**, and **Mage**.
2. **Promotion class, chosen around level 10** **⚠ verify**. You visit a promotion trainer and pick one of the **22 promotion classes** open to your base class, race, and (for some classes) gender.
3. **Disciplines, added later.** These are extra class-like packages that give more powers and skills. See [Disciplines](03-disciplines.md).

Your promotion class sets your main powers, the weapons and armor you can use, and your skill caps.

## Choosing a class

- **New players:** Warrior, Priest, and Ranger are forgiving and always wanted in groups.
- **Most wanted in guild PvP:** Healers (Priest, Druid, Prelate) and group support (Bard, Confessor, Doomsayer).
- **Solo PvP and ganking:** Assassin, Nightstalker, Huntress, Barbarian.
- **Scouting and intel:** Scout and Thief. Guilds value these highly for spotting enemy movements.
- **Sieges:** Mages with area-of-effect spells (Wizard, Warlock) and tough tanks.

## Tips

- **Plan your promotion before you create the character.** Some promotions need a specific race, gender, or base class.
- **Healers:** Put points into constitution too. Enemy groups kill healers first.
- **Hybrids** (Crusader, Nightstalker, Fury) are flexible, but only if you plan your stats carefully. Spreading points too thin makes a character weak at everything.
