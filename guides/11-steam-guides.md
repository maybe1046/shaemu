## Summary: "How to be Furious" (Fury build)

The author says the build is good for solo PvP, mines, banes, group-versus-group fights, power-levelling other players, and farming.

- **Race:** Irekei. They have good stats, good racial disciplines, and access to two heals. Fury gets **permanent flight**, so you don't need a race that can fly.
- **Starting runes:** −5 Strength, −5 Dexterity and −5 Spirit (to free up points), plus **Brilliant Mind**, **Warlord's Page**, and **Tough as Nails**.
- **Runes to get in game:** Amazing Constitution (15), Legendary Constitution (35), and Legendary Intelligence (35).
- **Disciplines:** **Bladeweaver**, **Runecaster**, and **Undead Hunter**.
- **Gear:**
  - 24 INT on every piece
  - A 165 ATR staff with 12 INT
  - Archmage's of Mastermind hood and robe
  - CC gloves
  - 40-stamina Windies
  - Two Peerless of Thunderbolts balanced daggers

For more Fury builds, see [Morloch Wiki: Fury Builds](https://morloch.shadowbaneemulator.com/index.php/Fury_Builds).

## Tips from Steam discussions

From the ["Getting Started" thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1475570/discussions/0/3112521650160375101/):

- **At level 10, teleport to Sea Dog's Rest.** It's a safehold city that exists on every server, and it's usually where players trade and hang out.
- **Buy stat potions there.** Get potions that raise your **Constitution** and your main stat. A few of these make a big difference on the newbie island.
  - A Greater Constitution Potion gives **+55 CON for 3,600 seconds (1 hour)**, but **doesn't stack** with CON buffs from healers or bards.
- **You can solo most of the beginner areas** and keep all the gold for yourself.
