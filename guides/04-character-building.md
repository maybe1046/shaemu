# 4. Character Building

Shadowbane lets you customize a character in great depth. The Steam listing mentions more than **200 attribute points** and **500 skill points**. That freedom also makes it easy to ruin a character, so plan first.

## The pieces of a build

1. **Race.** Sets base stats, stat caps, and special abilities. See [Races](01-races.md).
2. **Base class and promotion class.** Set your powers, skill caps, and the weapons and armor you can use. See [Classes](02-classes.md).
3. **Attributes.** Strength (STR), Dexterity (DEX), Constitution (CON), Intelligence (INT), and Spirit (SPI).
4. **Skills.** You raise these with training points at trainers, for example weapon skills, casting skills, defense, and stealth.
5. **Runes.** Stat runes permanently raise attributes or caps. Discipline runes add disciplines.
6. **Disciplines.** See [Disciplines](03-disciplines.md).
7. **Gear.** Weapons, armor, and jewelry with rolled bonuses, plus socketed gems on Lakebane.

## Attributes (general rules)

| Stat | Affects |
|------|---------|
| **STR** | Melee damage and how much you can carry. Required for heavy weapons and armor |
| **DEX** | Attack speed, chance to hit, and dodging. Important for archers and rogues |
| **CON** | Health and resistance to being stunned or disabled. **Every build needs some** |
| **INT** | Mage spell power and mana |
| **SPI** | Healer and holy spell power and mana, plus mana regeneration |

## Planning a build step by step

1. Open the [Character Builder](https://lakebane.com/calculator/).
2. Pick your race, base class, and promotion class.
3. **Meet the requirements first:** the stats and skills needed for your main weapon or powers and for the disciplines you plan to take.
4. **Put enough into CON.** A build that deals great damage but dies in two hits loses in PvP.
5. Put the remaining points into your main damage or healing stat.
6. Write down the order you'll train skills in at each level.
7. **Save the build** and share it with guild veterans for feedback before you start playing it.

## Training

- You gain **training points** as you level and spend them at **trainers**, found in cities. Different trainers teach different skills.
- **Train your main weapon or casting skill first.** Every fight uses it.
- Skills have caps set by your class, promotion, and disciplines, plus **⚠ verify** a limit on how many points you can spend per level.

## Common mistakes

- Spending all your points on damage and too few on health.
- Missing a discipline's stat requirement by a few points.
- Choosing a weapon type your promotion class can't train to a high level.
- Skipping the planner. **Always plan before you spend points.**
