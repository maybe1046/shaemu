# 9. Lakebane Server Notes

Lakebane describes itself as a Shadowbane emulator focused on **fast, fresh content, scout duels, and the economy**. It also runs on Steam under the name **Shadowbane: Resurgence**.

Always check the latest [patch notes](https://lakebane.com/patch_notes.html). This page is a summary and may be out of date.

## Known changes compared with retail

### Gear
- **Elite equipment:** stats were updated so most builds can use it.
- **Glorious weapons:** faster-attacking versions of the top weapons. They require **120 skill** to equip.
- **Gems:** equipment can be socketed with gems. There are 3 tiers: **Crude**, **Superior**, and **Epic**.

### Mines
- There's no distance penalty on mine production.
- **Universal mines** produce any resource type, based on a fixed gold value.
- **Mithril** comes only from mines and is needed for Epic gems.
- Everyone alive and within loading range when a mine is claimed gets a **1M-value Glass gift** (an item you destroy, or "junk", for its value), win or lose.
