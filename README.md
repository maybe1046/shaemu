# shaemu — Shadowbane Player Guides

Guides, tips and reference notes for playing **Shadowbane** on the modern emulator servers:

- **Lakebane** — the Shadowbane emulator at [lakebane.com](https://lakebane.com/), focused on fast, fresh content, scout duels and the player economy.
- **Shadowbane: Resurgence** — the Steam release ([store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4371680/Shadowbane_Resurgence/), released April 2026).

> **Accuracy note.** Shadowbane has had many patches, and each emulator server tunes things differently. These guides cover the fundamentals that have stayed the same. Anything marked **⚠ verify** is server-specific (numbers, restrictions, drop rates). Check it against the [Lakebane Character Builder](https://lakebane.com/calculator/), the [patch notes](https://lakebane.com/patch_notes.html), or Discord before relying on it. ## Quick tips (TL;DR)

1. **Items in your bags can be lost; equipped gear is safe.** When you die, other players can loot your inventory, but not what you're wearing. Bank gold and spare items often.
2. **Plan your build before you spend points.** Use the calculator. Retraining is expensive or restricted, and a bad stat spread can't easily be undone.
3. **Join a guild early.** Shadowbane is built around guilds and cities. Solo players have a hard time.
4. **Learn to spot trouble.** Keep an eye on the chat channels, notice unfamiliar names, and move off the roads when you see them.
5. **Carry recall items and use safe logout spots.** Don't log out in the open.
